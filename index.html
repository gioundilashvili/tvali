კონფიდენციალურობა

რას ინახავს აპი

მდებარეობა

GPS გამოიყენება მარშრუტის ჩასაწერად, GPS სიჩქარისთვის და ავტომატური ფარისთვის. თუ ნებას დართავ, მარშრუტი იწერება მაშინაც, როცა აპი დახურულია ან ეკრანი ჩამქრალია. ეს ნებისმიერ დროს შეგიძლია გამორთო ტელეფონის პარამეტრებში.

Bluetooth

Tvali: Privacy and Safety

Updated: 2026-11-02 · g.undilashvili@gmail.com

Privacy

Tvali has no accounts, ads, analytics or server. The app does not send your data to anyone.

What the app stores

On your phone: trips (time, distance, speed, battery, voltage, current, PWM, temperature and GPS route), settings, and the name and identifier of your device.

Location

GPS is used to record your route, for GPS speed and for the automatic headlight. If you allow it, your route keeps recording when the app is closed or the screen is off. You can turn this off at any time in your phone settings.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to connect to your wheel, scooter or BMS. The app does not use the microphone.

Other services

Route maps are shown by Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps (Android); the map service receives requests for the area you view. A trip file leaves the app only when you share it. Your phone backup (iCloud or Google) may include app data.

Deleting data

You can delete a trip in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone.

Safety

Tvali is a companion app, not a safety device. Alerts can be late or missing, and speed, battery and range are estimates. Wear protective gear, follow the law and do not look at your phone while riding.

Trademarks

Tvali is an independent app, not affiliated with Leaperkim, Veteran, Nosfet, Xiaomi or Segway-Ninebot. Names are used only to describe compatibility.
